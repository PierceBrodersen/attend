One visit with Attend

  1. You talk with your patient

    Tap start on your phone and set it down. Attend captures the conversation, with the patient's consent, and nothing else.

  2. Attend drafts the note

    By the time you stand up, a structured note is waiting: history, exam, assessment and plan, in your template and your voice.

  3. You review and sign

    Every line links back to the moment in the visit it came from. Edit anything, then sign it into the chart.
